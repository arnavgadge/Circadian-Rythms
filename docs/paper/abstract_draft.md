# Abstract (Draft)

**Status: draft.** This document is a methodology/results draft toward an eventual IEEE-style paper; it is not a finished or submitted manuscript.

**Release version:** v1.0.0-research-demo. **Phase 17 audit status:** CONDITIONAL PASS.

---

Continuous, low-burden estimation of circadian phase from behavioral and environmental proxies — sleep timing, light exposure, screen use, meal timing, exercise, and caffeine intake — is of general interest because direct laboratory measurement of Dim Light Melatonin Onset (DLMO), the standard circadian-phase marker, is expensive, intrusive, and impractical for continuous or large-scale monitoring. This draft presents CircadianIQ, an end-to-end research architecture that estimates behaviorally-derived circadian phase and closes the loop with alignment scoring, deterministic behavioral recommendations, observational effectiveness measurement, and personalization, gated by a dedicated safety layer. Because no paired real-human behavioral/DLMO dataset was available to this project, the system is developed and evaluated entirely on a synthetic dataset (1,000 participants x 30 days) generated by a three-layer intrinsic-adapted-current DLMO state model. Circadian phase is predicted from a 68-feature, six-day trailing behavioral window using two independent XGBoost regressors (predicting sin(DLMO) and cos(DLMO)), fused with an independent state-engine estimator. On a strictly participant-held-out synthetic test set (200 participants, 4,800 windows), the model achieved a mean absolute error of 24.47 minutes, with 93.73% of held-out synthetic test windows within ±60 minutes of the synthetic ground truth. This substantially outperforms two non-learned baselines evaluated on the identical test set. We report these results, together with cross-validation stability, baseline comparisons, and an explicitly caveated fusion diagnostic, as evidence that the modeling pipeline behaves as designed on synthetic data. **We state prominently and without qualification that this system has never been validated against real human DLMO measurements, that all reported metrics describe performance against the project's own synthetic generative model, and that no result in this work constitutes a clinical, diagnostic, or real-world accuracy claim.** This draft documents methodology and synthetic-data results only, as a foundation for future real-human validation work.
